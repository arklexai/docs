The **Settings** page manages your personal profile and your organization's team, API keys, usage, and billing. Go to **Settings** in the sidebar. The page has five tabs: **Profile**, **Members**, **API keys**, **Usage**, and **Billing**.

---

## Profile

The **Profile** tab updates your account details:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Profile photo | Click your photo, choose a JPEG, PNG, or WebP file under 2 MB, crop it, and click **Apply**. |
| Full Name | Your name as it appears in evaluation results and shared views. |
| Company | The name of the organization you're working in. Only Owners of that organization can edit it. |
| Email | Your account email. Read-only. |
| Role | Your role in the current organization. Read-only. |

Click **Save Changes** to save your edits.

## Members

The **Members** tab shows everyone in your organization and lets Owners manage access.

| Role | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full access, including inviting and removing members, creating API keys, changing the plan and spending cap, editing the company name, running and approving MCP tools, and resolving annotations. |
| **Member** | Access to agents, scenarios, simulations, evaluations, knowledge, metrics, and MCP servers. Can view the team list, usage, and billing, but can't manage members, change billing, or create API keys. |

**Invite a teammate**: click **Add New Member**, enter the **Email Address**, choose a **Role** (**Owner** or **Member**), and click **Send Invitation**. The invitee gets an email with a link to join. People without an account can sign up from the invite. Invitations expire after 7 days.

**Manage invitations**: Owners can open the **Invited** list to see pending and expired invitations. Open the menu on an invitation to **Copy Invite Link**, **Resend Invite** (which restarts the 7 days), or **Revoke Invite**.

**Remove a member**: Owners can choose **Remove from organization** from the menu on a member's row. The member loses access right away. Their past work, such as evaluations and annotations, stays in the organization. API keys they created stop working. If this was their only organization, they get a new personal organization.

**Leave an organization**: if you belong to more than one organization, click **Leave Organization** on the **Members** tab. You'll need a new invite to rejoin. An organization's only Owner can't leave it.

## API keys

The **API keys** tab lets Owners create keys for the [Arklex Platform API](/api) and for [tool call tracing](/tool-tracing). Members see a message asking them to contact an Owner.

1. Click **Create key**.
2. Enter a **Name** (up to 100 characters) and click **Create key**.
3. Copy the key from the **Copy your API key** dialog, then click **I have copied it**. This is the only time the key is shown.

The keys table shows each key's name, the start of the key, when it was created, and when it was last used. To revoke a key, click its trash icon and confirm. Revoking takes effect immediately.

A key acts for your whole organization. It can't access billing, members, or key management. Keys don't expire, but a key stops working if the Owner who created it leaves or is removed from the organization.

## Usage

The **Usage** tab shows how much your organization has used Arklex over a date range. Choose **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days** (the default), or **Last 12 Months**.

- **Tokens Consumed** and **Compute Time**: totals for the range.
- **Token Usage Over Time**: a chart of input and output tokens.
- **Breakdown by Type**: runs, input tokens, output tokens, and compute time for **Simulation**, **Scenario Generation**, and **Evaluation**.

## Billing

The **Billing** tab shows your current plan, your token usage for the billing period, and your overage controls. Only Owners can change the plan or the spending cap.

### Plans

|  | Free | Pro | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | \$0/month | \$299/month | Custom |
| **Best for** | New organizations | Production workloads | Custom scale, compliance, or on-prem |
| **Input tokens** | 700K per period | 14M per period | Custom |
| **Output tokens** | 100K per period | 2M per period | Custom |
| **Knowledge tokens** | 5K (lifetime) | 100K (lifetime) | Custom |
| **Concurrent operations** | 2 at a time for each type of operation | No plan limit | No plan limit |
| **Overage** | None. Operations pause at the limit. | \$0.01 per 1K input tokens, \$0.05 per 1K output tokens | Custom |
| **Overage ceiling** | None | \$2,000/month default, with a custom spending cap | Custom |
| **Support** | Email | Email | Dedicated CSM and SLA |
| **Extras** | None | None | Bring your own LLM, on-prem deployment |

### Token usage

Three meters track your usage. Each bar turns amber at 80% (**Approaching limit**) and red at 100% (**Allowance exceeded**).

| Meter | Resets | Counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input tokens | Each billing period | Tokens Arklex's models read: the simulated user, scenario generation, and the LLM judge |
| Output tokens | Each billing period | Tokens Arklex's models write: the simulated user, scenario generation, and the LLM judge |
| Knowledge tokens | Never | Tokens used by the documents and websites currently in your knowledge base |

Your agent's own token usage isn't counted. The tab shows the current billing period's start and end dates. On the Free plan, each billing period lasts 30 days.

Knowledge tokens are a hard limit on every plan, including Pro. When you reach it, you can't add more knowledge. Deleting documents or websites frees their tokens. Contact support to raise the limit.

### Overage and spending cap

On the **Free** plan, operations pause when you reach any token limit. No overage is charged.

On the **Pro** plan, operations continue past the included tokens, and overage is billed at the rates above. The tab shows the estimated extra charge for the current period, billed at the end of the period. Overage is capped at \$2,000 per month by default.

To set a lower cap, click **Adjust cap** under **Spending cap**. Enter a monthly limit up to \$2,000. Enter \$0 to block all overage, or leave it empty to use the default. When your overage reaches the cap, new simulations, evaluations, and scenario generation pause until the next billing period.

### Manage your subscription

- **Upgrade to Pro**: opens Stripe checkout.
- **Manage subscription**: opens the Stripe billing portal, where you can update your payment method and download invoices.
- **Cancel subscription**: click it, then **Continue to cancel** to finish in the Stripe portal. Your Pro plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. To undo a scheduled cancellation, click **Reactivate**.
- **Contact us**: for Enterprise pricing.

<Note>
  Stripe handles checkout, payment methods, and invoices. Arklex doesn't store your card details.
</Note>

## Account menu

Click your organization name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu. From there, you can:

- Switch between your organizations under **Organizations**.
- Choose a **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**.
- **Log out**.
- See the version of the app you're using. Include it when you contact support.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I belong to multiple organizations?">
    Yes. Accept an invite to join another organization, then switch between organizations from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar. Your role can be different in each organization. You can't create new organizations yourself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to data when a member is removed?">
    Evaluations, simulations, agents, and scenarios they created stay in the organization, and their annotations and comments remain attributed to them. They lose access, and any API keys they created stop working.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a Member invite others?">
    No. Only Owners can send invitations. Members can view the team list but can't see pending invitations or add and remove people.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
