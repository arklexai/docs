Annotations let your team score evaluation results with their own judgment, alongside the LLM judge's automated scores. Reviewers enter their own values, Owners resolve disagreements, and the resolved scores show how well the judge agrees with your team. You can export the results to CSV for stakeholders who aren't on the platform.

<Note>
  Annotations are available on evaluations that score your agent. Simulated-user evaluations don't support annotations. See [Evaluations](/evaluations) for how to run one.
</Note>

---

## How annotations work

An agent evaluation has an **Annotations** section: a spreadsheet with one row per agent turn (or per conversation) and one column per metric. Each cell shows the judge's score below the input where you enter your own.

Reviewers annotate **independently**. Each person sees the judge's score and enters their own value without seeing other reviewers' scores, which prevents anchoring bias.

What you see depends on your role:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Owner view">
    Owners see other reviewers' scores in each cell: the first two reviewers by name or initials, and the rest under **+N more**. When more than half of the reviewers chose the same value, Owners also see a **Majority** value. Owners can resolve rows to settle disagreements.

    <Frame>
      ![Annotations section in the Owner view, showing reviewers' scores side by side](/images/annotations-owner-view.png)
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Member view">
    Members see only their own scores, which keeps their judgment unbiased. Once an Owner resolves a row, the resolved score is visible to everyone.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Resolved rows turn green and show a **resolved** badge. Rows you've edited but not saved show **unsaved**, or **incomplete** if some metrics are still empty.

## Choose an annotation scope

A toggle at the top of the section switches between two scopes. They're independent, so an evaluation can have annotations at both levels:

| Scope | What you annotate |
| --- | --- |
| **Turn** | Individual agent turns. Use this to pinpoint exactly which response was a problem. Only turns the judge scored appear. |
| **Conversation** | The conversation as a whole. Use this for metrics that only make sense across a full exchange. |

Large evaluations load in pages. Click **Load More** to see more conversations.

## Dispute a judge score

When you think the judge scored something wrong, find the row and enter your own score for each metric. The input depends on the metric:

- **Whole-number scale**, such as 1 to 5: a row of numbered buttons. Click a number to select it, and click it again to clear it.
- **0 to 1 scale**: a text box that accepts one decimal place.
- **Labels**: a dropdown of the metric's labels.

You must score every metric in a row before you can save it. When you're done, click **Save Annotation** at the bottom right. It saves all the rows you edited in the current scope. Unsaved changes stay in your browser tab until you save, and the browser warns you before you leave the page.

### Annotate from a conversation

You can also annotate while you read a transcript. Open a conversation from the **Conversations** section. Under each agent turn, click the annotation link (**not annotated**, **annotated**, or **resolved**) to open the **Annotate** tab for that turn. To annotate the whole conversation, click the link beside **Metrics** at the top. Score the metrics, then click **Save Annotation**.

## Resolve disagreements

<Info>
  Only Owners can resolve annotations. If you belong to several organizations, your role can differ in each one.
</Info>

Resolving produces one resolved score per metric. A value wins only when more than half of the reviewers chose it.

| Action | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Resolve** | Each row in the spreadsheet | Resolves the turn or conversation by majority. Enabled once every metric in the row has at least one annotation. |
| **Resolve Turn** | **Annotate** tab of a conversation | Opens **Resolve by Majority Vote** with the majority values filled in. Metrics with a tie are marked **Tie - required**, and you pick their value. You can change any value before you click **Confirm Resolution**. |
| **Resolve Conversation** | **Annotate** tab of a conversation | Resolves conversation-level annotations by majority. |
| **Resolve All** | Top of the section | Resolves every turn and conversation at once. On a tie, it picks one of the tied values at random. It skips rows that are missing an annotation for any metric, and reports what it skipped. |

<Tip>
  To settle a tie on a turn yourself, use **Resolve Turn** instead of **Resolve All**.
</Tip>

Resolved rows are read-only for everyone, including Owners. To change one, click **Unresolve**, edit the scores, and resolve the row again.

## Annotation calibration

The **Annotation Calibration** section shows how well the judge agrees with your team's resolved scores:

- **Overall Agreement**: the share of resolved scores where the judge agreed with your team.
- **Need Attention**: how many metrics have less than 75% agreement.
- **Coverage**: how many turns and conversations your team has resolved.
- **Evaluator Calibration**: agreement for each metric, lowest first. Quantitative metrics also show the mean absolute error (**MAE**).

For quantitative metrics, the judge agrees when its score is within 1 of the resolved score. For label metrics, the labels must match exactly. Agreement uses resolved turns only. Resolved conversations count toward **Coverage** but not toward agreement. Reload the page to see the latest numbers after you resolve more rows.

<Frame>
  ![Annotation Calibration with agreement per metric](/images/annotation-calibration.png)
</Frame>

Use calibration to find the metrics whose prompts need work, then refine them on the [Metrics](/metrics) page.

## Add comments

Comments are separate from scores and are for free-form discussion. Everyone with access to the evaluation can see them. Open them from the **Comments** button at the top of a conversation.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Conversation comment">
    Type in the box (up to {commentChars} characters) and press Enter. Press Shift+Enter for a new line.

    <Frame>
      ![Adding a comment on a whole conversation](/images/annotations-convo-comment.png)
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Turn comment">
    Click **comments** under an agent turn. The header changes to that turn, and your comment applies to it.

    <Frame>
      ![Adding a comment on a specific turn](/images/annotations-turn-comment.png)
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You can't edit or delete a comment after you post it.

## Annotation history

In a conversation, the **History** tab for a turn shows:

- The judge's scores.
- The resolved scores, if the turn is resolved.
- Each reviewer's latest annotation, with when it was last updated.

Members see only their own annotations. Saving a new value replaces your previous one.

<Frame>
  ![Annotation history panel showing scores with reviewer and timestamp](/images/annotations-history.png)

## Export to CSV

Click **Export CSV** at the top right of the **Annotations** section to download the annotation data for the evaluation. The file has one row per reviewer annotation, with each reviewer's scores and the resolved scores. It doesn't include the judge's scores or comments.

Anyone in the organization can export the file, and it includes every reviewer's scores. Use it to share results with people who aren't on the platform, such as subject-matter experts or compliance reviewers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can annotate?">
    Anyone in the organization can annotate. Only Owners can resolve, unresolve, and bulk-resolve.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to annotations if I re-evaluate?">
    Annotations belong to the original evaluation. **Re-evaluate** creates a new evaluation with no annotations, and the original and its annotations stay as they are.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a limit on reviewers per evaluation?">
    No. Any number of team members can annotate the same evaluation independently.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
